Adapted from React Bits Sling Button TS/CSS for the compact studio Top control.

The upstream rubberband pull, tension band, loaded arc, launch-velocity spring,
and particle burst are retained at a smaller scale. This site uses a fixed vertical
axis with slight horizontal give and 36px pad inside a 64×48px control. Unused
customization props and icon dependencies are omitted. Native tap and keyboard
activation also play a small launch; reduced motion returns without an animation.
Pointer cancellation, hidden-page cleanup and pending animation cancellation are
handled locally. The label remains legible while the arrow follows the pull.
