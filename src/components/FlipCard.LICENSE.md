Adapted from React Bits Flip Card TS/CSS for the studio portrait, signature and riding photo.

The official spring flip, pointer drag with velocity snapping, hover tilt, moving shadow, and two backface-hidden planes are retained. This site uses a fixed vertical axis and responsive card dimensions; unused configurable axes, glare, online demos, and controlled-component APIs are omitted. Portrait and riding colors remain unchanged. Disabled chapters and hidden pages stop and settle animations; reduced motion swaps faces without rotation.
